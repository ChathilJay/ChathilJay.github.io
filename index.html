AI Chatbot for Clinical Psychology

Capstone · Project Lead & Scrum Master

Psychology trainees need a safe, repeatable way to practise diagnostic conversations. We built a simulator where an LLM plays patients whose mood shifts during the session.

60patient personas 6person team 12month delivery
  • Engineered a 60-persona prompt library with multi-step prompting on a locally hosted LLaMA model, and solved CPU and token-throttling bottlenecks so patients stayed realistic and responsive.
  • Combined LLM reasoning with a Q-learning agent and deterministic session logic to vary patient mood, a hybrid AI and rule-based design.
  • Shipped the full stack (React SPA, Python REST API, MongoDB, Nginx) to the NECTAR research cloud with Docker and CI/CD.
  • Worked with the clinical client for a year through demos, user testing and handover.
